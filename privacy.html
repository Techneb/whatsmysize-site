Privacy policy

What's my size? · Last updated 24 October 2026

What's my size? is made by AlephB. It works out your clothing size from clothes you already bought. Your data stays in your own Google account, in your browser, or on your computer. AlephB has no server for this software and never receives your data. It has no analytics, tracking or ads.

Google data it uses

  • Gmail, read-only: a sync program on your own computer reads the order and return emails in one Gmail label you choose. It never sends, changes or deletes mail. The browser extension uses Gmail access only to check that the Gmail API is turned on.
  • Google Sheets: your purchases, size charts, fit notes, saved outfits and wishlist are kept in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. The extension reads and writes that spreadsheet only.
  • Google Drive, only the files it creates: outfit pictures you like are saved to a folder it creates in your Drive. It cannot see your other files.

What is sent to AI providers

Some features call Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) using API keys that you add yourself. Without a key, those features are off and the rest still works.

  • Reading order emails: the email text and product photos go to Anthropic to list the garments you bought. Your name, address, email address, phone number and card digits are removed first.
  • Size charts shown as images: the image goes to Anthropic so it can read the measurements.
  • Finding and checking garment photos: the brand, item name and colour go to Anthropic for a web search. Candidate photos go to Gemini to check they show the right garment.
  • Outfits and shopping ideas: garment descriptions and photos from your spreadsheet go to Gemini, and search requests go to Anthropic.
  • Reading orders from a shop account, only when you click it and in a tab where you signed in yourself: the page text, with personal details removed, goes to Anthropic.

Each provider handles this data under its own API terms.

What stays in your browser

Product pages are read in your browser to find the size chart. Settings, your API keys and a copy of your spreadsheet are kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.

Limited Use

What's my size?'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google data is used only for the features above.
  • It is shared only with Anthropic or Google Gemini, and only for the feature named above.
  • It is never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Nobody reads it, and AlephB never sees it.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes what it keeps in your browser. Your spreadsheet and outfit folder are yours to keep or delete in Google Drive. You can revoke its access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
